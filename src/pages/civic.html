Civic information

Who governs Lyons, and where the official information is

Lyons is a statutory town under Colorado law, governed by an elected mayor and six trustees. It runs its own utilities, parks and planning; Boulder County, two special districts and the school district provide the rest.

This section is a neutral route to the official bodies. ExploreLyons.com is not one of them and takes no position on Town business. Last verified {{today_long}}.

The Town Board

Mayor and Board of Trustees

Seven members, all elected at large by the town's voters for two-year terms in April of even-numbered years. The mayor presides and votes; a mayor pro tem is chosen by the Board from among the trustees. A Town Administrator runs the staff day to day.

Regular meetings
First and third Mondays, 7 pm

Shirley F. Johnson Council Chamber, Lyons Town Hall, 432 5th Avenue. Meetings are hybrid, with a livestream and remote public comment. A holiday Monday moves the meeting, usually to Tuesday; the agenda, posted the week before, is the authority. Public comment on items not on the agenda is taken near the start.

Advisory bodies

Boards and commissions

Residents appointed by the Board, most meeting monthly at Town Hall or the library. Openings are advertised on the Town site and filled by application.

A separate authority, with the Board as its commissioners, that manages tax-increment financing for downtown redevelopment.

Who does what

The Town, the county and the districts

Incorporation in 1891 means the Town has the powers a Colorado statutory town gets: its own utilities, streets, parks, zoning, building code and sales tax. It contracts out policing and belongs to districts for fire, the library and schools.

Town of Lyons

Water, wastewater and electric utilities; streets and snow; parks, the campground and recreation; building permits, planning and zoning; the municipal court; the April municipal election; and the Town's sales and property taxes. Town Hall, 432 5th Avenue, 303-823-6622.

Boulder County

The Sheriff's Office polices the town under contract from a substation on Railroad Avenue. The county runs public health, property records and assessment, motor vehicles, county roads outside town, the open-space parks around it, and, through the Clerk & Recorder, every election but the Town's.

bouldercounty.gov ↗

Special districts

Fire and emergency medical response come from the Lyons Fire Protection District, the library from the Lyons Regional Library District, and schools from the St. Vrain Valley School District. Each is a separate public body with its own elected board and its own mill levy.

Voting

Elections in Lyons

Two systems run side by side. The Town Clerk conducts the regular municipal election on the first Tuesday of April in even-numbered years, by mail ballot, for the mayor and trustees and any Town ballot questions. The Boulder County Clerk & Recorder conducts everything else: the November coordinated elections for county, school-district, library-district, state and federal offices, and any Town question the Board chooses to place on a November ballot.

Voter registration is statewide through the Colorado Secretary of State; a registered Boulder County voter with a Lyons address is automatically on the Town's rolls. The county's ballot drop box for the area is listed on its elections page before each election.
