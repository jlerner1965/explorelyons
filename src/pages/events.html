About this calendar

Confirmed dates, without the clutter.

This is a curated list, not the whole calendar. The Town’s community calendar carries every public meeting and Town event; the Lyons Recorder lists what the venues, the library and the clubs publish. An entry is added here only after its date has been read on one of those pages.

The year in Lyons

Annual events

The fixtures that come round each year. Exact dates are set by the organizers and go into the upcoming list above once they are published; until then, the month is the best guide.

February
Old Man Winter Bike Rally & Run. Mixed-terrain 50K and 100K rides and a 10K run out of Bohn Park, with a party at the finish. Town events page ↗
Valentine’s Day
Lyons Polar Plunge. A charity dip in the St. Vrain by the Lyons Polar Bear Club.
March
Lucky Lyons 5K & 10K. A St. Patrick’s-season run through town.
April
Earth Day Celebration. Town-organized clean-ups and a festival in the park.
June
Lyons Outdoor Games. Trail running, kayaking, mountain biking, dog events and a beer garden across Bohn Park and the whitewater park.
June to August
Sandstone Summer Concert Series. Free concerts in Sandstone Park, downtown, on summer evenings.
Late July
RockyGrass. Three days of traditional bluegrass at Planet Bluegrass; the 2026 festival ran July 24–26. bluegrass.com ↗
Mid-August
Rocky Mountain Folks Festival. The songwriter festival on the Planet Bluegrass ranch; the 36th ran August 7–9, 2026. bluegrass.com ↗
Summer and fall
Artisan Markets. Town-run markets of Colorado makers, usually in Bohn Park; the fall market was September 12, 2026.
Late October
Halloween Spooktacular. Trick-or-treating and a costume parade along Main Street.
Early December
Parade of Lights. The holiday parade down Main Street, followed by the tree lighting.

The weekly rhythm

Standing dates at the taprooms and Town Hall. Each is in the month view above; a holiday or a private event can pre-empt one, so the venue’s page is the last word.

Sunday
Sunday Serenade, Oskar Blues, 5–7 pm. No cover.
Monday
Open Mic, the Rock Garden, 6–9 pm, weather permitting. Board of Trustees, Town Hall, 7 pm on the first and third Mondays.
Tuesday
Bluegrass Pick, Oskar Blues, 6–8:30 pm. Trivia, MainStage Brewing, 6:30–8:30 pm.
Thursday
Open Bluegrass Pick’n, MainStage Brewing, 6–9 pm.
Friday and Saturday
Touring and local acts at Oskar Blues, MainStage, the Rock Garden and Spirit Hound; see each venue’s page.
