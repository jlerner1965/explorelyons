How this directory is compiled

A curated list, not a census. A business goes in when its name, address and hours can be read on its own site or on the Town of Lyons visitor pages, and it carries the date that was done. Phone numbers appear only where the business publishes one. Nothing here is paid for, and no listing is ranked above another; groups are alphabetical. Send additions and corrections through the contact page with a link to the source.
