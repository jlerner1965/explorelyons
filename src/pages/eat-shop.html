How this directory is compiled

A curated list, not a census.

  • A business goes in once its name, address and hours can be read on its own site or the Town’s visitor pages — and it carries that date.
  • Phone numbers appear only where the business publishes one.
  • Nothing here is paid for. No listing outranks another; groups are alphabetical.
