Volunteer and membership organizations. Each line says what the group actually runs, so you know which one to approach for what.

  • 01

    Lyons Chamber of Commerce

    The membership organization for local businesses, at 350 Broadway: the member directory, the visitor center in Sandstone Park with the Town, and business promotion through the year.

    Runs: Member directory, visitor center, business events
    lyons-chamber.com ↗
  • 02

    LEAF — Lyons Elevating All Fund

    The town's mutual-aid nonprofit, founded as the Lyons Emergency & Assistance Fund: the Wednesday food pantry, Meals on Wheels, basic-needs grants, mental-wellness and recovery support, home check calls for seniors, and Lyons Volunteers.

    Runs: Food pantry, Meals on Wheels, emergency assistance, volunteers
    leaflyons.org ↗
  • 03

    Lyons Community Foundation

    A community foundation that grants to local nonprofits and projects each fall (applications in September, awards in October) and funds the arts commission's public-art program among others.

    Runs: Annual grants, community fundraising
    lyonscf.org ↗
  • 04

    Lyons Historical Society

    Formed in 1973 to save the 1885 railroad depot from being moved out of town; opened the Lyons Redstone Museum in the 1881 schoolhouse in 1979 and runs it still, with a research library and archives.

    Runs: Redstone Museum, archives, local history programs
    lyonsredstonemuseum.com ↗
  • 05

    Planet Bluegrass

    A private company rather than a club, but the organizer of the two festivals that define the town's summer, RockyGrass and the Rocky Mountain Folks Festival, and of the concerts on the ranch between them.

    Runs: RockyGrass, Folks Festival, ranch concerts
    bluegrass.com ↗
  • 06

    The Lyons Recorder

    The town's community news site: meeting reports, the fullest local events calendar, a community-resources directory and the churches list.

    Runs: Local news, events calendar, resource directory
    lyonsrecorder.org ↗

Public bodies with a Lyons remit

Public administration, not clubs: each is part of, or appointed through, government, and is approached as such.

  • Public body

    Town of Lyons

    The municipal government: the Board of Trustees, Town Hall at 432 5th Avenue, and the departments that run water, sewer, electric, streets, parks, the campground, building permits and planning.

    Runs: Utilities, parks, permits, planning, elections
    lyonscolorado.com ↗
  • Public body

    Lyons Arts & Humanities Commission

    A Town commission of appointed residents that curates the heARTS of Lyons sculpture collection, runs the annual call for artists and supports arts programming, with a Town arts coordinator.

    Runs: Public art collection, call for artists
    Public art, Town of Lyons ↗
  • Public body

    Lyons Regional Library District

    A special district with its own elected board, funded by a property-tax mill levy. It opened the library at 451 4th Avenue in 2019 after the flood put the Depot library out of use; programs most days, a meeting room and a foundation that raises for it.

    Runs: The library, programs, meeting rooms
    lyons.colibraries.org ↗
  • Public body

    Lyons Fire Protection District

    The fire and emergency-medical district for the town and the surrounding canyons, staffed around the clock, with an auxiliary that does outreach and preparedness before, during and after emergencies.

    Runs: Fire, EMS, wildfire preparedness
    lyonsfire.org ↗
Where community happens

A town that rebuilt itself once already.

When the St. Vrain flooded in September 2013 it cut every road in and out, took the water, sewer and power, and put a fifth of the housing out of use. LEAF fed people, the library ran from a trailer, and the parks, bridges and utilities came back over the next six years. Most of the groups on this page were shaped by that.

Who to contact for what

Lyons is an incorporated town, so most everyday services come from Town Hall. The rest come from Boulder County, the fire and library districts and the school district. Each row names the body responsible and links to the page of its site that handles that service.
