The small print

Privacy, sources and photo credits

This is a static site. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The fonts and the map software are served from here; the only outside requests are the map tiles, when you open a map, and the river reading.

Privacy

What is collected

Browsing. Nothing. The pages are plain files.

The web host keeps ordinary server logs (IP address, page requested, time) for security and capacity, and deletes them on its own schedule. There are no cookies, no analytics scripts, no advertising and no embedded content from other sites.

Links marked ↗ leave for another site, which has its own policy.

Submissions. The contact form sends what you type, and your email address if you give one, to the editor.

It is used to check the submission and reply about it, kept as long as the listing it supports is on the site, and never shared or used to send anything else. If the form is not yet connected to a mail service, it says so and nothing is sent.

Directory and events. Only public business information is published: a name, a public address, published hours, a public phone number where the business publishes one, and a link.

No personal names appear in the directory. Anyone named on the civic page is named in their public office, as the Town publishes it.

The map. The maps on Eat & Drink, Stay and Trails & River load only when you open one. Opening a map fetches tiles from OpenStreetMap, whose servers see your IP address and which tiles you view, under the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

The river reading. With scripting on, the home and Trails & River pages fetch the level from the Colorado Division of Water Resources’ public streamflow service. That request carries your IP address to a State of Colorado server.

Neither is used here to identify you, and both pages work without them.

Removal. A business or organizer can have a listing changed or removed by writing to editor@explorelyons.com.

Sources

Where the facts come from

ExploreLyons.com is an independent guide — not the Town of Lyons, Boulder County, the Chamber, or any other body.

Every page links to the official source it relies on, and the directory and calendar carry the date each entry was checked.

The main sources are the Town of Lyons site (lyonscolorado.com), Boulder County, the Lyons Recorder, the Lyons Redstone Museum, Planet Bluegrass, the businesses’ own sites, and — for the town’s history — Wikipedia’s article and the sources it cites.

Where a fact could not be verified it is left out or marked as unverified rather than approximated. Corrections with a source are welcome through the contact page.
