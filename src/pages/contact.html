What goes in

Verified, credited, dated.

A listing needs a name, an address and a page of its own where hours can be read. An event needs a date on the organizer's page. A correction needs the source that shows the current fact. Each goes in with the date it was checked.

Businesses
Free, and never ranked. Any Lyons business with a public address and its own page.
Events
Public events in or about Lyons, once the organizer has published the date.
Public art
Works go in with a verified title, artist and location, and the artist's credit.
Photographs
The photographs on this site are credited on the photo credits list. Photographers who would like their work of Lyons used here, with credit, are welcome to write.
Not here
Anything official: permits, utilities, complaints, records requests. Those go to the body responsible.
