Under the cliffs

Camping in town

The Town runs a campground inside LaVern M. Johnson Park, a five-minute riverside walk from Main Street, with the whitewater park, the splash pad and the playgrounds on the doorstep.

  • RV sites with full hookups, open year-round.
  • Tent sites from mid-April to mid-October.
  • Reserve through the Town — popular weekends fill early in the year.

Campervans
Dry camping for self-contained campervans at the St. Vrain Trailhead on 2nd Avenue, mid-May to mid-September, also through the Town.
Bears and fire
Bears walk the river corridor: food in the vehicle or the locker, never the tent. Fire restrictions apply in dry spells; the county posts the current stage.
Further out
Roosevelt National Forest dispersed camping begins in the canyons west of town; St. Vrain State Park (19 miles) and Boyd Lake State Park (27 miles) have year-round campgrounds with hookups.
RockyGrass and the Folks Festival

Festival weekends: plan the bed first

Late July and mid-August bring several thousand people to a town of two thousand.

  • On-site camping at Planet Bluegrass sells with the festival tickets, and goes fast.
  • The tiny homes and the lodge book out months ahead; the Town campground follows.
  • Nearest hotel beds are in Longmont, twelve miles east — then Estes Park, twenty west.
01Buy the festival camping with the ticket if you want to sleep on the ranch.
02Otherwise book in Lyons the week the dates are announced, usually the winter before.
03Missed both? Longmont hotels, and the festival shuttles from Bohn Park.

When Lyons is full

Beds within half an hour

Lyons has no hotel. The nearest are east along Colorado 66 and US 36, and west in Estes Park — all an easy drive back for dinner and the show.

Longmont

12 miles · 15 min

The most beds and the widest range of prices: the chain hotels cluster along Ken Pratt Boulevard and the Diagonal, a straight run on Colorado 66.

Visit Longmont lodging ↗

Estes Park

20 miles · 30 min

Mountain lodges, cabins and the Stanley, at the gate of Rocky Mountain National Park. Busy all summer; the drive down the canyon is part of the trip.

Visit Estes Park lodging ↗

Boulder

17 miles · 20 min

City hotels and the airport shuttle connection. US 36 runs straight from downtown Boulder to Main Street.
